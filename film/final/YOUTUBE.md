## Title
iTantra: speak and send with no network | SIH26173 | Team chmod 777

## Description
iTantra lets Android phones pass a spoken message along with no tower, mobile data, Wi-Fi or internet. Speech becomes text on the phone, without internet, and the text hops phone to phone over Bluetooth LE, encrypted at every hop, so relays can't read it. We showed 6 hops on real phones, and the phone at the end reads the message aloud. SOS reaches anyone nearby, with no saved contact needed. One speech model covers 9 Indian languages in 697 MB, and it all runs on an ordinary mid-range phone with stock Android and no new hardware.
