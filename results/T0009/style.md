# Caption style proposal

For the 1920 × 1080 master, set captions in Segoe UI Variable at 46 px, weight 600, with 1.16 line height. Use off-white `#F3F5F8` text and a near-black `rgba(5,7,10,0.82)` rounded rectangle with 20 px horizontal and 12 px vertical padding. Center the caption block horizontally, cap it at 1200 px, and allow at most two lines of 42 characters each. Keep a 42 px bottom margin.

This places a two-line caption block at approximately y=907–1038. Scene 1's top panel ends at y=318; its right panel ends at y=830. The captions therefore sit below both panels. Keep the same bottom position when either panel disappears.

Apply this style in the compositor; SRT carries text and timing only. Keep captions off the empty phone shape after speech ends at 53.06 s.
