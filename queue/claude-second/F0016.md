## Goal
One restrained music bed across the whole film, made offline (no downloads, no copyrighted music), plus a mix plan the assembler can follow.

## Details
`film/music/`: `make_bed.py` that writes `RENDERS:music/bed_v3.wav` (48 kHz stereo) from a cue sheet `film/music/cues.json` (section starts in film seconds, so it can be re-run when the timeline changes). Default cue sheet (edit later): intro 0-25 (very quiet, warm pad, no beat), s2a 25-75 (light pulse under the walk and overhead, quiet under speech), s2b 75-110 (sonar: low hum + slow pulse, matches the existing sonar sfx in key/feel), s3 110-155 (a little more tension: lower register, slightly faster pulse, no siren, no booms), exploded 155-172 (glassy, airy), cards 172-186 (rises gently, resolves on a clean chord on the closing card, ends with the fade).
Sound: soft pads/sines/filtered noise with slow movement, one key throughout (choose one and say which), no drums beyond a soft pulse, no trailer hits. Crossfade every section change over 2-3 s so it is one piece.
Also `film/music/duck.py`: given the bed and a dialogue stem (wav, same length), writes a ducked bed: -10 dB under speech (detect from the dialogue stem's envelope with 150 ms attack / 600 ms release), extra -4 dB under the app's TTS windows listed in `cues.json` (`tts_windows`). And a one-page `film/music/MIX.md`: target levels (dialogue around -18 LUFS short-term, bed around -30 LUFS under speech, -24 between lines, film -16 LUFS integrated, true peak <= -1.5 dBTP).
Render a 60 s excerpt to `results/F0016/preview/bed_excerpt.mp3` (128 kbps, under 2 MB) covering the s2b to s3 change so the lead can hear the tension step.
