## Goal
Replaces the old portrait map card. At the end of the sped-up walk the camera pulls up into a LANDSCAPE overhead view of the campus with pins at Vachana and Yash and "~300 m, walking distance", then zooms into Yash's pin onto his real shot.

## Details
Write a self-contained module in `film/scene2/v3a/overhead/`: `overhead.js` (API: `Overhead.mount(el, opts)`, `Overhead.render(t)`; t = seconds inside the 5.0 s segment; pure function of t, no clocks, no random), `overhead.css`, and `preview.html` (a 1920x1080 HyperFrames composition that mounts it for 5 s so you can render stills). Draw the campus from the local OSM data in `film/scene2/geo/` (`nie_north_osm.json`, `campus_outlines.json`; reuse the projection code idea from `film/scene2/mapcard/geo.js`): buildings as soft extruded light-grey shapes, paths as thin lines, green areas muted, on a dark warm-neutral ground; no basemap tiles, no internet. Pins: "Vachana" and "Yash" (`.label-pin`, Vachana blue dot, Yash white dot) at the same two points the v2 map card used (read them from `film/scene2/mapcard/` / v2 code); a thin dashed walking line between them with the text `~300 m, walking distance` in a `.glass-panel` (exact string). Keep the real distance between the pins consistent with ~300 m on the map scale; if the v2 points measure very differently, report the measured distance instead of faking it.
Motion (5.0 s): 0-0.8 s the walk still (`opts.walkStill`) shrinks and tilts back into the ground plane near Vachana's pin while the map fades up around it (feels like the camera rising); 0.8-3.4 s overhead settles, pins pop in, the line draws from Vachana to Yash, the distance label appears (readable at least 2.5 s); 3.4-5.0 s push into Yash's pin, the map dissolves into `opts.yashStill` which fills the frame exactly at t = 5.0 (identical to that image at full frame so the scene can cut to his live clip).
Use the glass kit (`film/common/glass/`, copy at build) or the class names with a fallback if it isn't in git yet. Text exactly as in spec section 3.
Previews: extract the two stills yourself for testing (normalpart2 last second, normalpart6 src 0.0) to a git-ignored folder; render stills at 0.0, 0.6, 1.5, 2.6, 3.4, 4.2, 4.97 s to `results/F0006/preview/` (960 px) and check them at phone size.

## Done when
Module and preview committed; stills show a readable landscape overhead with both pins and the label, a smooth rise from the walk and a match to Yash's frame at the end.
