## Goal
Vivek wants the exploded view as a REAL phone teardown, photoreal. Step 1: three layer images of the same phone, made with your built-in image generation tool (`image_generation`). If you have no image generation tool in this session, say so in the first line of REPORT.md and finish with `status: failed` immediately (the lead then uses another route).

## Details
Generate three separate images of ONE generic modern Android phone (no brand, no logos, no Apple-like design: flat-edged aluminium frame, punch-hole camera), all in the SAME camera view and scale: a three-quarter top view (about 30 degrees tilt, 20 degrees turn), soft studio key light from top left, subtle rim light, photoreal product-render quality, each on a TRANSPARENT background (PNG with alpha; if transparency is not possible, a flat pure black background), at least 2048 px on the long side:
1. `layer_screen.png`: the front glass + display panel alone (black display, glossy glass edge with a soft reflection), as it would lift off the phone.
2. `layer_mid.png`: the metal mid-frame with the internals clearly visible: green/black circuit board with a processor chip (shielded SoC), a small separate wireless chip with antenna traces/flex lines near the top edge, a large battery, a bottom loudspeaker module with grille, a tiny microphone at the bottom edge, connectors and screws. Realistic, detailed, but not cluttered.
3. `layer_back.png`: the matte back cover (dark grey/graphite) with the camera bump, seen from the same angle as if the other layers were removed.
Generate each with the same written description of the phone, view and lighting so they match; regenerate any that does not match the others in angle or proportions. Then make `stack_preview.png`: the three composited in their order with even gaps (simple offsets) to check they line up.
Part map: for `layer_mid.png`, write `results/F0030/parts.json` with the pixel centre and a rough bounding box of: microphone, processor, bluetooth_chip (the small wireless chip), antenna (one point on the antenna line), loudspeaker, battery. Check them by drawing labelled boxes on a copy (`parts_check.jpg`).
Output: the PNGs and parts_check.jpg go to `local/private-out/F0030/` AND a copy of each to `RENDERS:exploded_v2/` (they are generated art, not footage or deck material, but keep full-size PNGs out of git). Commit only 960 px JPG previews of each image plus `parts.json` and `parts_check.jpg` (960 px) in `results/F0030/`.

## Done when
Three matching layers + the stack preview + parts.json exist; REPORT says which tool generated them and anything that does not match.

## Never
Logos, brand names, Apple design, text in the images. Frame-by-frame video generation. Footage or deck files.
