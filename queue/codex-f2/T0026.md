## Goal
The project is supposed to have `brief/style.md` (colours, fonts, motion speed, never-do rules) so every scene matches. It was never written because the look was approved scene by scene. Draft it from what is actually in the built scenes, so later fixes stay consistent.

## Details
Read the compositions and templates under `film/` (scene1, scene2/main, scene2/sonar, and scene3/title if present) and `film/scene1/grades.sh`. Extract and document, with the file each value comes from:
- Colour tokens: text, secondary text, panel backing, accent teal, relay blue, SOS red, sonar campus tones, dim/overlay values.
- Typography: families, the `local()` font-face pattern, sizes and weights for each role (eyebrow, headline, body, small label, callout text, title card), letter-spacing.
- Panel style: backing opacity, radius, border, shadow, blur.
- Motion: durations and eases for reveals, step cues (the 0.12 s lead before a spoken word, 0.3 s reveal), punch-ins (8%), the doorway morph, callout line draw, float amplitude.
- Sound: loudness targets (-16 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP), where music is allowed, stem rules.
- Colour grade: `GRADE_V1` and where it applies (camera clips only, never app screens).
- The never-do list, gathered from the briefs and handovers.
Flag every inconsistency you find between scenes (for example two different blues, two caption sizes, different panel radii) in a short table with a suggested single value.
Write `results/T0026/style.md` (the lead copies it to `brief/style.md` after review).

## Done when
`style.md` is complete enough that a new worker could build a matching panel from it alone, and the inconsistency table exists.

## Never
Edit files outside your `writes`. Commit media over 8 MB, footage, audio sources or fonts. Absolute paths, user names or emails. URLs or downloads. "Demo"/debug labels in anything that ships.
