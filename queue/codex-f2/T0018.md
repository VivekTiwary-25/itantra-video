## Goal
Write the script the lead will run on vivek-pc to make the full film: `film/final/assemble.py`. You can't run it on the real renders (they are on vivek-pc), so build it against synthetic test clips you generate with ffmpeg (`testsrc`, `sine`), and prove it works on those.

## Details
Inputs, all under `RENDERS:` (= `renders_dir` from `machine.local.json`), with the paths configurable at the top of the script:
- `scene1/scene1_draft_v3.mp4`: video plus its final voice mix (scene 1 has no music).
- Scene 2: a video source (`scene2/scene2_draft_nomusic.mp4`), plus `scene2/scene2_dialogue_sfx.wav` and `scene2/scene2_music.wav`.
- Scene 3: a video source (`scene3/scene3_draft.mp4`), plus `scene3/scene3_dialogue_sfx.wav` and `scene3/scene3_music.wav`.
- `title/title.mp4`: video with its quiet tick audio.

The script must:
1. Check that every input exists, and report its duration, resolution and fps. Fail clearly if any video is not 1920x1080 at 30 fps.
2. Join the videos with hard cuts. Avoid re-encoding where possible; if the streams differ, re-encode once at high quality (libx264, CRF 16, yuv420p, bt709 tags).
3. Build three audio files on the film's full timeline: `dialogue_sfx` (all speech, narration, app audio and sfx), `music` (only the music, silence elsewhere), and the mix of the two. The music stems arrive already ducked; don't add more ducking.
4. Set the mix to -16 LUFS integrated with true peak at or under -1.5 dBTP (two-pass loudnorm, linear). Apply the same gain to the no-music version so the dialogue level is identical in both.
5. Write `full_film_v1.mp4` (with music), `full_film_v1_nomusic.mp4` (the same picture, dialogue_sfx only) and `full_film_v1_music_only.wav`. Audio AAC 256k, 48 kHz stereo, `+faststart`.
6. Write `full_film_v1_report.json` with each scene's start and end time in the film and the measured loudness.
7. Print a scene-boundary check: the last frame time of each scene and the first of the next, so the lead can confirm that the phone-shape frame at the end of scene 1 matches the first frame of scene 2.

No absolute paths in the script; read `machine.local.json`.

## Done when
`assemble.py` runs end to end on your synthetic inputs, the outputs have equal durations, and REPORT.md shows the test run's output and the exact command for the lead.

## Never
Bake music into the dialogue layer. Commit media. Absolute paths. URLs.
