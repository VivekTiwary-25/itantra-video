## Goal
Every card and panel in film v3 uses one frosted-glass look (it replaces the old blotchy blurred-video cards). This kit is used by every other v3 task, so it is first and should be small and solid.

## Details
Build `film/common/glass/` exactly to the contract in section 1 of the spec: `glass.css`, `glass.js`, `noise.png` (a small tileable grain texture you generate with Python, 256x256, used at ~3% opacity), `README.md` (how to use; the build-time copy rule), and `demo/index.html` (a 1920x1080 HyperFrames composition, 4 s, showing every class over real footage).
Look (dark glass, Apple-like, quiet):
- `.glass-card`/`.glass-panel`: `backdrop-filter: blur(var(--glass-blur)) saturate(1.4)`, blur ~28 px, tint rgba(14,18,26,0.48), 1 px border rgba(255,255,255,0.18), a faint top inner highlight (linear gradient white 8% to 0), radius 28 px (cards) / 22 px (panels), soft shadow 0 24px 60px rgba(0,0,0,0.35), grain overlay. Text white `--text` #f3f5f8, dim `--text-dim` rgba(243,245,248,0.7). Card title 64 px semibold; panel text 40/30 px. Must be readable over BRIGHT daylight footage (test on `FOOTAGE:Video/normalpart1.mp4` at src 2.0 s and on `FOOTAGE:Video/vachna part1.mp4`).
- `.glass-card.full`: covers 1920x1080, radius 0, tint opacity 0.86 so the backdrop barely shows.
- `.glass-circle`: light matte glass: tint rgba(255,255,255,0.55), blur 30 px, border rgba(255,255,255,0.6); content on it is dark (#14181f).
- `.techline`: top centre, y = 64 px, pill, 30 px text, padding 12x26, max width 1300 px, one line (second smaller line allowed: `.techline small`, 22 px, dim).
- `.caption`: bottom centre, baseline at y = 1000, 38 px, white with a soft dark text shadow plus a subtle dark glass strip behind (tint 0.35), max width 1400 px, max two lines.
- `.label-pin`: small pill label, 26 px.
- Colours: `--blue` #4da3ff (Normal), `--red` #ff4d5e (SOS), `--gold` #f5c451 (Bluetooth glow).
- `glass.js`: `Glass.phone`, `Glass.icon`, `Glass.cross` as in the contract. The phone: 1080x2400-ratio screen with 44 px radius at screen height 1000 px, a thin near-black bezel (14 px), a punch-hole camera, a faint edge highlight. Icons: simple 2 px-stroke line icons, drawn by you as inline SVG (no icon fonts, no downloads). `Glass.cross(el, p)`: a red-white strike line drawn diagonally across the icon as p goes 0 to 1.
Also write `brief/style.md` (one page: the tokens above, fonts, sizes, caption and tech-line rules, the "no frozen frames" rule, blue/red/gold meaning), so later tasks can read it.

Preview: render 5 stills from the demo (each class over footage, plus the phone with a placeholder screen and the icon row with two crossed) into `results/F0003/preview/*.jpg` at 1920 px, plus one 960 px contact sheet. Look at them at phone size (scale to 480 px wide) and confirm the card and panel text still reads; write what you checked.
