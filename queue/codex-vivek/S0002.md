## Goal
The T0007 drafts show placeholders for every app slot, but one real take already exists on this PC: `RENDERS:scene2/app/vachana_send.mkv` (1080x2400, with audio, recorded with scrcpy). Raw phone recordings need preparing before they can go into a slot. Build that step once as a tool, so the lead can drop in the remaining recordings after 14:00 with one command. Then use it for `vachana_send`, put the map fallback in `maps`, and re-render both drafts.

## Details
1. **Write `film/scene2/main/prep_slot.py`.** Usage: `python film/scene2/main/prep_slot.py <slot> <raw file under local/renders/> --in <s> [--dur <s>] [--crop-top 110] [--audio keep|drop]`. It writes `local/renders/scene2/app/<slot>.mp4`:
   - crop the top `--crop-top` px (default 110; this removes the hotspot pill in the status bar)
   - trim from `--in`, for `--dur` (default: the slot's `duration` in `slots.json`)
   - constant 30 fps, H.264 yuv420p (high quality, CRF ~16), even dimensions
   - **no grade and no colour change**
   - audio as AAC 48 kHz if `keep`, no audio track if `drop`
   - It should also accept `.mkv`/`.mp4` inputs with variable frame rate (scrcpy output is VFR).
   - Make `build.py` robust to a slot whose aspect isn't exactly 1080x2400: fit to full height and keep the aspect.
2. **Put in `vachana_send`.** Read the tap times in `local/app/vachana_send.log` (ptt_down about 1.5 s, ptt_up about 6.6 s, then the recipient, Yash, and Send; use the logged values).
   - Vachana's clean spoken line sits at 0.182-4.762 s into the slot. Choose `--in` so that **ptt_down lands about 0.1 s before 0.182 s** in the slot, i.e. `--in ≈ ptt_down - 0.08`.
   - Use `--audio drop`, because her voice already comes from the clean dialogue track and must not double.
   - Set the slot `duration` to (Send tap - in) + 1.0 s, rounded to whole frames, and set `sent_at` to (Send tap - in) + 0.15 s.
   - Set `ptt_down` and `ptt_up` in `slots.json` to their in-slot times.
   - Later segments and narration will shift; that is expected.
3. **Put in `maps`** with the fallback card:
   - `cd results/S0001/mapcard_portrait && hyperframes.cmd render -q high -f 30 -o ../../../local/renders/scene2/app/maps_fallback.mp4`
   - Point `slots.json` `maps.path` at `RENDERS:scene2/app/maps_fallback.mp4` (3.5 s, no audio). Don't edit `results/S0001/`.
4. Re-run `python film/scene2/main/build.py`, and render both drafts to the same names as T0007 (`RENDERS:scene2/scene2_draft_music.mp4` and `_nomusic.mp4`). Loudness -16 LUFS, true peak -1.5 dBTP. `hyperframes check` must pass.
5. Save 6 stills (960 px JPG) to `results/S0002/preview/`: the dissolve into `vachana_send`, the moment of her PTT hold with her line, the Send tap, the frame after the freeze, `maps`, and one sonar frame. Also write the new timeline table in REPORT.md.
