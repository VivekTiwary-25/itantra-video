## Goal
Vivek wants every beat looked at, at full size and at phone size, before a full render is accepted. Make that one command.

## Details
`python film/final/beat_stills.py [--film RENDERS:full_film_v3_draft.mp4] [--report <assembly report json>] [--out <dir>]`:
- Beat list: for each segment in the assembly report (start time in the film), read that segment's `timeline.json` (intro_v3, scene2/v3a, scene2/v3b, scene3/v3, exploded_v2, cards_v3: whatever keys they use for beats/segments/labels; handle each format) and take one time per beat (middle of each segment/beat, plus the first and last frame of every segment = the joins).
- Extract each frame with ffmpeg at full size (JPG q2) and make two contact sheets: `beats_full.jpg` (960 px tiles, labelled with film time + segment + beat name) and `beats_phone.jpg` (480 px tiles, same labels). Also `joins.jpg`: last frame of each segment next to the first frame of the next.
- Default out dir `RENDERS:review/<film name>/`. Print the paths.
- Test it on a tiny synthetic film you make with ffmpeg plus fake reports (you have no real renders on your laptop). Add a section to `README_v3.md`.
- Also add `--beats-only` to print the beat table without extracting.
