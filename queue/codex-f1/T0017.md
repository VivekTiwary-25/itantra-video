## Goal
Build the closing title card as a HyperFrames composition in `film/title/` (1920x1080, 30 fps). The text and tone are in `discussion/scene3/001-claude-lead.md`, section "Title card". This is the last thing the judges see: calm, confident, clean.

## Details
- **Look:** a dark background (near-black with very subtle depth, e.g. a faint radial lift; no noise texture, no particles). Type: Segoe UI Variable via `@font-face src: local('Segoe UI Variable Display'), local('Segoe UI')`, exactly as in `film/scene1/index.html.tpl`. Near-white text (#f3f5f8). The soft teal accent #8fe3d4 may be used sparingly (e.g. for "iTantra" or a thin rule). No red, no blue.
- **Motion:** sentences 1-4 appear one at a time, each fading in (about 0.6 s, a few px of rise), with a calm pause. Choose timings so each line can be read comfortably at normal speed (roughly 3.0-3.5 s per line). Decide whether earlier lines stay on screen dimmed or clear away; pick what reads best and say why. Then line 5 ("iTantra. Speak. Send. Be heard.") appears on its own, larger, and holds. Line 6 (the team line) is small at the bottom and fades in with line 5. Hold the final state for 3 s.
- **Distance:** `{DISTANCE}` in line 3 comes from a single constant at the top of a small `build.py` (default "~400 m") that fills `index.html` from `index.html.tpl`. Name the template `.tpl`, not `.html`, or `hyperframes check` sees two compositions.
- **Sound:** a soft tick as each line lands, generated procedurally (Python/numpy, deterministic), very quiet, from `film/title/make_sfx.py`, which writes a stem. No whooshes, no music.
- Local GSAP only (copy `film/vendor/gsap/gsap.min.js` next to the composition). No URLs.
- Run `hyperframes.cmd check`, snapshot each line's frame into `results/T0017/preview/`, and render a 540p preview mp4 (under 8 MB) there.
