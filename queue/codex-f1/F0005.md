## Goal
The film's opening (~15-18 s): Vachana's short intro to camera, with the idea shown beside her on frosted glass, word by word. Read spec section 2 (intro) and section 3 (exact text).

- Camera: `FOOTAGE:Video/20261002_081346_short_intro.mp4` (13.3 s, South Campus, in front of the stairs).
- Clean voice: `FOOTAGE:Audio/vacchna_short_intro.mp3` (15.3 s). A first ASR pass heard: "I'm Vachana from team chmod 777 for ISRO's problem statement, SIH26173. We built iTantra, speak(?) ..., message travels from phone to phone, no network needed. Let's see how it works." Use what she really says, never the original script where it differs.

## Details
1. Audio prep (`film/intro_v3/prep.py`): find the clean audio's offset to the camera audio by cross-correlation (report it and the correlation peak). Word timings with faster-whisper (`local/models/faster-whisper-medium.en`, word_timestamps, beam 5, `initial_prompt` = the line above) on the clean audio; write `film/intro_v3/words.json` and report the words with times and probabilities. Voice check: is she full or tinny? Measure the spectrum (energy below 300 Hz, 300-3k, above 3k, spectral centroid) against the cleaned scene 1 voice (the output of scene 1's `build_voice` or its source chain applied to `FOOTAGE:Audio/vachna part1.mp3`); if she is clearly thinner, apply scene 1's chain (and at most a gentle low-mid shelf, +2-3 dB around 150-250 Hz) and report before/after numbers. Also an RMS envelope of the cleaned voice at 30 fps to `film/intro_v3/envelope.json` for the waveform. Rule B: audio only from 0.12 s before her first word to 0.30 s after her last word, 30 ms fades; camera audio never used. Output `RENDERS:intro_v3/voice.wav`.
2. Picture (`film/intro_v3/`, HyperFrames, 1920x1080, 30 fps): her take full frame, graded `GRADE_V1`, playing at real speed, NEVER frozen. She stays uncovered in the left part of the frame: look at frames, and if she isn't already in the left ~45%, shift the picture (scale up to at most 1.15x and offset) so she sits left with the stairs behind. All graphics live in the right ~45%, on glass, word-timed from `words.json`:
   - On "I'm Vachana ... SIH26173": a `.glass-panel` with `Vachana` / `Team chmod 777` / `Problem statement SIH26173` (exact). It slides in softly and stays until "We built iTantra", then makes way.
   - On "speak" (or "We built iTantra" if "speak" isn't there): a waveform (a bar or line waveform driven by `envelope.json`, white with a faint blue) on a glass strip.
   - On "message": the waveform settles onto a flat baseline and her words type out along it, word by word at her timings (`message travels from phone to phone`, as she says them).
   - On "travels ... phone to phone": the typed text tightens into a compact message card (`.glass-panel`, small, with a tiny lock), which arcs into an Android phone mockup (`Glass.phone`) showing `home.png`; a banner drops down from the top of the phone screen: app icon dot + `New notification` (exact).
   - On "no network needed": the phone floats onto a `.glass-circle`; above/around it a row of icons: `Tower`, `Mobile data`, `Wi-Fi`, `Internet`, `Bluetooth LE` (labels exact, 26 px, dark on the light circle); the first four get crossed out one by one (`Glass.cross`) on the words; Bluetooth stays, with a soft gold glow (`--gold`), never crossed.
   - On "Let's see how it works": the circle grows (1.2-1.5 s, power2.inOut) to fill the frame and becomes the full card: `<div class="glass-card full"><h1>How the app works</h1></div>`, h1 centred in the frame (exact text; scene 2's first frame uses the same markup and must match). Hold the full card 0.5 s with the text fully visible; the intro ends there. If her clip runs out before the card covers the frame, the card must already cover it (no frozen camera ever).
   Keep at most two graphic elements fully visible at once; everything readable at phone size; motion soft (no bounces).
3. Captions for her line from `film/captions/v3/intro.json` (generate it from `words.json`, her exact words), `.caption` style, bottom centre; they must not collide with the right-side graphics (move the graphics up if needed).
4. Sound stem `RENDERS:intro_v3/sfx.wav`: one soft glass tick when the panel lands and when each icon is crossed, very quiet (same family as `film/exploded/ticks.py` if it exists), a soft whoosh under the card growth. No music.
5. `render.cmd` that builds and renders to `RENDERS:intro_v3/intro_v3.mp4` given machine.local.json (the lead runs it over SSH), plus `timeline.json` (beat times).
Previews: stills at every beat (panel, waveform, typing, card in flight, phone + banner, icons with two crossed, all four crossed + Bluetooth glow, circle growing, full card) as 1920 px JPGs in `results/F0005/preview/`, plus a phone-size sheet (each 480 px wide); every line must read at 480 px. A 540p preview mp4 (< 10 MB) via snapshot + ffmpeg if feasible.
