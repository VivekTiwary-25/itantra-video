## Goal
Nobody on the team can watch the film in real time or hear it, so every render must pass a measured check before Vivek sees it. Write `film/final/qc.py`.

## Details
`python film/final/qc.py <video> [--report out.json]` must measure and print, then exit non-zero on any FAIL:
- Container and streams: 1920x1080, 30 fps constant, H.264 yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz stereo; video and audio durations within 50 ms of each other.
- Loudness: integrated LUFS (target -16, tolerance 1), true peak (at or under -1.5 dBTP), and short-term loudness per 10 s window so a dead-quiet or too-loud stretch shows up.
- Picture: black frames (ffmpeg `blackdetect`), frozen frames longer than 1.0 s (`freezedetect`; report them, since holds are sometimes intended), and any frame that is a flat colour.
- Audio: silence longer than 1.5 s (`silencedetect` at -50 dB; report with times), clipping (samples at full scale), a left/right channel mismatch.
- Placeholder guard: sample one frame per second and flag frames that look like the placeholder slot card: a dark portrait card with the grey text `APP: <slot>` on it (see `film/scene2/main/index.html.tpl` for its exact colours and position; detect it by that fixed layout and colour, no OCR).
- Output a readable table and the JSON report.
Test it on synthetic clips you make with ffmpeg (a good one, one with a black gap, one with silence, one too loud, one with a fake placeholder card) and show each result.

## Done when
`qc.py` catches every planted problem in your synthetic tests and passes the clean clip; REPORT.md shows the runs and the exact command.
